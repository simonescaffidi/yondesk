macOS — first launch. The app isn't signed/notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it on first launch (“can't be checked for malicious software” / “damaged”). It's not a virus: it's Apple's check on apps from outside the App Store. To open it:
  1. Drag Yondesk into the Applications folder.
  2. Right-click the app → Open → Open. (Only needed the first time.)
  3. If it still says “damaged”, open Terminal and run:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Yondesk.app then relaunch.
