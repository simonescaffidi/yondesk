No second device at hand? See in 15 seconds what a support session looks like: consent, remote screen, operator cursor, chat and file transfer.
Note. In the browser, control is for demonstration (live screen, operator cursor, chat, files, clipboard). Real mouse/keyboard control of the OS requires the native app, always with visible consent and a session banner.
