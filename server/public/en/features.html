Features

As powerful as the big names, transparent by design

Everything you expect from a professional remote support tool.

🖥️ Real-time screen control

View the remote computer and control its mouse and keyboard with minimal latency, from the desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux.

📱 Control from your phone

In the Android or iOS app your phone screen becomes a touchpad: tap to click, long-press for right click, drag, plus a keyboard.

📁 Two-way file transfer

In the web client you send files both ways: they travel over the encrypted peer-to-peer channel, never through servers or cloud.

💬 Session chat

In the web client, built-in messaging to coordinate with the user without external tools.

📋 Send clipboard

Copy text on your computer and type it on the remote one in a click: handy for links and commands.

🛡️ Unattended access (opt-in)

For your servers and PCs: you enable it on the device, with a dedicated password and an always-visible icon/banner showing access is enabled.

🔒 Security

End-to-end encryption (DTLS-SRTP), per-session password, mandatory consent on the assisted side, lockout after failed attempts.

🌐 Cross-platform

Windows, macOS, Linux (desktop app), Android and iOS (mobile app) and a web client with no install. The clients connect to each other; on Android you can also share your phone screen.

🔗 Direct connection

WebRTC with STUN/TURN: peer-to-peer when possible, relay when NAT traversal is needed.
